# Roadmap

The order to build `game.md` in. Each step ends with something you can
sit down and play for an evening, and each points at the document that
plans it; the order matters, and the detail is the plan's. The milestones
of 2026-10-10 and the brief that ran them are in `archive/plan.md`.

## Built

Roads and streets, one-way; buildings placed whole from the build menu,
sites that wait for their timber and stone and go up as the vans bring
it, and taking down what is new as its undo; residents with needs and
commutes; depots, the farm, the sawmill and the quarry, and calls for
stock by road; islands in one ocean; the ferry harbour as the one door,
its deck booked a slot at a time or pinned, and the order board's
letters (`trade.md`, `missions.md`); missions; the sea's harness and
real routes (`plan-sea.md` stages 1, 2 and 5); the interchange
(`plan-interchange.md` §Built); and the server on a VPS (`deploy.sh`).

## Next, in order

1. **The market's first version** (`plan-market.md` §The first version):
   a world price per good that moves, freight from the distance, the
   market tab, tanks and depots as what you hold.
2. **The sea's network** (`plan-sea.md` stages 3, 4 and 6): a second
   harbour, lines that follow the traffic, the container port.
3. **Chains in town** (`buildings.md`): the mine, the cement works, the
   steelworks, the refinery, and the depots that hold much of one
   class; charters for tankers and bulkers after them (`shipping.md`).
4. **Roads that are roads**: the interchange's priority, lanes and levels
   in the town, roads faster than streets (`plan-interchange.md`).
5. **Power** (`game.md` §Power): the plant, pylons, substations.
6. **Services and the panel** (`game.md` §Conditions and services): fire
   and health as calls, the inspect panel.
7. **Other people** (`multiplayer.md`): towns on the other islands, trade
   between them by sea.
8. **The advisor** (`game.md` §Legibility).

Later, if play asks: houses that climb a ladder of wants, the airport,
sidings and trains, nuclear, the police.
