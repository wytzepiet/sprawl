The UI's faces (`client/ui/ink.css`), as the page loads them from
`/fonts`: Inter at 500, 600 and 700 for words, and
Nunito at 700 for numbers and titles. Quicksand (500, 600) and Fraunces are the
interchange's, in the page (`src/app.css`). All are under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org): github.com/rsms/inter,
github.com/googlefonts/nunito, github.com/andrew-paglinawan/QuicksandFamily
and github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces.
